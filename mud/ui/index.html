    Dank Mud Client

    A MIP-native client for 3Kingdoms.

    Where everything lives. The program folder is replaced wholesale by an update; nothing you own is in it.

    Free software under the GNU General Public License v3. Includes xterm.js under the MIT licence. Source and releases.

    Map & bot updates

    The map and the route library come from jmitchell33/3kdb, a TinTin++ setup for 3K that keeps growing. Taking an update only ever adds: rooms you do not have, exits you do not have, routes you have not named. Nothing you have walked, renamed or edited is touched.

    Only the map, the named destinations and the bot library are pulled — all of them data. Your scripts are Python and hot-reload, so they are deliberately not on this list.

    Triggers

    Fire when the MUD sends a line that matches.

    Aliases

    Fire when you type something — a short word that stands for one or more commands.

    Events

    Fire on something happening — a tell, a channel line, a combat round — already parsed into named fields by MIP, so there is no pattern to write and nothing to break when a wizard recolours a name.

    Stat watches

    Fire when a number crosses a line — health, spell points, the enemy's health. Once on the crossing, not on every update: 3k.org sends two per combat round.

    Timers

    Send something every so often. Kept on the game's own two-second beat rather than the wall clock, and held back the same way everything else is when the minute is busy — a timer is not a reason to trip the APM ceiling.

    Typing /tick 290 xp makes one of these, and /ticks lists them. /delay 5 quaff heal is the one-off version and is not kept.

    Panels

    What is on screen. Nothing floats: the messages sit across the top of the output and the map at the top of the sidebar, so there is nothing to place and nothing over the text.

    Show
    Terminal

    3k.org draws to about eighty columns — its own map is 78 wide and prose wraps well short of that — so a wide window leaves a lot of empty terminal. The cap gathers that space into one piece on the right, where the floating panels sit, rather than spreading it across every line. It never bites on a narrow window.

    It stays there selected: Enter sends it again, and typing anything replaces it.

    Numpad

    With something typed in the box, the keys type numbers as usual — unless this says always. Holding a key down sends once.

    Any command, or several separated by ;. Leave a key empty and it types its number.

    Sounds

    Dings are chosen in the messages window: right-click a line and pick every … line for its channel, or anything from … for that person. A tell chimes twice, a channel once. Your own lines never ding, and nor does a channel you have hidden. 3K's own bell — what somebody's wake sends you — rings with three notes.

    Kept in this browser, not on your character, so a second window can be laid out differently.

    Messages window

    In the terminal's font. Smaller by default, so a conversation fits.

    Kept in this browser, not on your character. Everything else on screen grows and shrinks with Ctrl + and Ctrl −; Ctrl 0 puts it back.

    Character setup

    Settings on your character, not changes to the mudlib.

    Line markers

    3K can wrap each kind of line in a marker of your choosing, which turns its output from prose into fields with two ends. With them set, this client can name the room you are in about twice as often. You never see them: they go in for the parser and come straight back out before the screen.

    The first click prints the settings so you can read them. The second sends them.

    edits live in scripts/prefixes.json
    Your own colours

    The markers stay on your character if you go back to another client. Save your colour settings first — 3K's ansivars page, read and kept for this character — and they can be put back afterwards.

    Brief mode

    3K's own brief setting. Short descriptions show a room's one-line title instead of its paragraph; the minimap is the one 3K draws beside it. This client draws its own map, so you may not want both.

    Script files

    Rules registered from Python files in scripts/. They run in the same engine as everything above; edit the file to change one.

    New route

    The walk

    Directions separated by spaces, commas, semicolons or new lines. 3n repeats a step. Anything that is not a direction is sent as a command — climb pipe, enter. Wrap a step in { } to keep something containing a separator together.

    A room number. The client walks you there first and checks it arrived. Leave blank to start wherever you are.

    Commands sent once on arrival, one per line.

    The hunt

    A name, or any word from the long description — archangel catches all ten. Separate with commas. Leave blank to walk without fighting.

    How it runs

    Off by default. A route that quietly starts over is one still walking your character around an hour after you stopped watching.

    seconds between steps

    New rule

    When

    All conditions must hold. Leave empty to fire every time the event happens.

    seconds

    Checked on the game's beat, so anything under two seconds is a number the client cannot keep. Nothing fires until MIP is flowing: before that the beat free-runs, and the login prompt is no place to send commands.

    Otherwise it fires on every update while the condition holds, and 3k.org sends two per combat round.
