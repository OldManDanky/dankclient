# Sounds

[Options → Sounds](options:sounds) sets what each thing sounds like.

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| A tell to you | a tell, from anybody you chose to ding for |
| A channel line | a channel you chose to ding for |
| 3K's bell | somebody used `wake` on you |
| A bot ends by itself | a route finished or gave up (not when you stop it) |
| You have been idle | the deadman trips |
| The MUD drops you | the connection is lost (not when you disconnect) |

Each can be **built-in**, **no sound**, or your own file: press **Upload…** and pick an mp3, wav, ogg or m4a up to 2 MB. **▶** plays it; **×** throws your file away. Your files are kept with your map, not in the browser.

Choose which tells and channels ding by right-clicking a line in [the messages window](#messages).
